# MeetingGuard V2: plan from week 6, two tracks (Dusko and Sven)

Written 2026-10-22, after weeks 1 to 5 shipped (Android 1.5.0 live, iOS 1.5.0 in Apple review,
WhatsApp moved to the client number). Weeks 1 to 5 are listed only for the record.

Two tracks run side by side from week 6:

- **Track A, the app (Dusko).** The agreed roadmap, unchanged in content and order.
- **Track B, the website (Sven).** A world-class marketing site, designed from scratch, three
  weeks. From week 9 Sven joins the app track; what he takes is listed per week.

Dates keep one week per row from October 26. Cost is deliberately left out of this file: the
agreed stages are covered by the existing agreement, and anything new is priced by Dusko and
Sven with Francisco.
