## 4. The AI executive assistant (new)

You hand the assistant a matter, by voice or text, and it runs that matter by email until it is done: it writes to the people involved, follows up, books the meeting and turns promises into tasks, always inside limits you confirm. The attached proposal lists every task it performs and the rules it follows. Our best price is USD 4,950, USD 1,100 less than on October 26, because version 1.6.0 already has the AI allowances and the store subscriptions it builds on.

| Part | Milestones | Price | Who | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Production | A5 to A8 | USD 2,200 | Sven A5 to A7, Dusko A8 | About 4 weeks after your go on production |

| Total | 9 milestones | USD 4,950 | Sven USD 3,850, Dusko USD 1,100 | About 10 weeks from your first go, plus the days you take to decide after the demonstration |

Production starts only after you have seen the prototype work and said go. If you stop after the prototype, nothing more is charged.

The assistant works in English and Spanish; it does not follow the app into Portuguese, French and German. Not in this price, for a later phase (part 3 of the proposal): other languages, whole-inbox triage, assistant workflows on WhatsApp, shopping, travel or restaurant booking, and a continuous phone-call style voice. Sending from your own mailbox is built for Microsoft accounts. Sending from Gmail needs Google's approval and a yearly security assessment paid to the assessor; until then Gmail users use the assistant's own address, which works for everyone from day one.

The estimated running cost is about USD 0.46 a month for a typical subscriber and USD 1.96 for a heavy one, plus an email provider account of about USD 16.50 a month shared by all subscribers (part 6 of the proposal).
