## 4. The AI executive assistant (new)

You hand the assistant a matter, by voice or text, and it runs that matter by email until it is done: it writes to the people involved, follows up, books the meeting and turns promises into tasks, always inside limits you confirm. It lives in the app, where you talk to it, answer its approvals and get its notifications on your phone, and on the web agenda, where you delegate by typing, answer approvals, follow your matters and read long email threads on a larger screen. The attached proposal lists every task it performs and the rules it follows. Our best price is USD 5,500, USD 550 less than on October 26, although the assistant now also comes to the web agenda. The price is lower because version 1.6.0 already has the AI allowances, the store subscriptions and the voice the assistant builds on (part 4 of the proposal).

Sven builds the assistant, first in the app and then on the web agenda, and sends each part to Dusko for review; Dusko checks it, tests it and adds it to the app or the web agenda, so nothing reaches your users without his review. Until the beta in A10, the assistant stays switched off in the store releases of the agreed scope. Dusko then takes the assistant through the notifications, the subscription, the security review and the store release.
